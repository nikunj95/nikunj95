**Selected work**

- [AkidaTag](https://github.com/Brainchip-Inc/AkidaTag): firmware for BrainChip's ultra-low-power AIoT board, with on-device keyword spotting and learning, and model and firmware updates over Bluetooth. I led this work.
- [BrainChip Connect](https://github.com/Brainchip-Inc/BrainChip-Connect): the companion phone app for BrainChip's Akida AKD1500 boards, such as AkidaTag. I am its main developer.
- [Symphony Community Akida Bundle](https://github.com/Brainchip-Inc/symphony-akida): brings BrainChip's Akida chips into IBM Spectrum Symphony. I led the release and wrote most of the code.
- [BrainBoard1500 Arduino library](https://github.com/Brainchip-Inc/brainboard1500_arduino_library): runs Akida AKD1500 models on Arduino Nicla boards. I added the Nicla Voice keyword spotting demo, and human detection to the Nicla Vision demo.
- Open source: contributions to [gh-axi](https://github.com/kunchenguid/gh-axi) and [pi-code](https://github.com/ilovepixelart/pi-code), tools for AI coding agents.
- Teaching: founded BrainChip's University AI Accelerator Program in 2022, starting with a Carnegie Mellon University course.
